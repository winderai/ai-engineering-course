Chapter 1: Introduction to Brewing
Beer brewing is an ancient art that combines science and creativity. The process begins with malting, where grains are soaked and allowed to germinate. This creates enzymes that will later convert starches into fermentable sugars.

Chapter 2: The Mashing Process
Mashing involves steeping the malted grains in hot water. The temperature must be carefully controlled between 148-158°F. This activates enzymes that break down complex starches. The resulting liquid, called wort, contains the sugars needed for fermentation.

Chapter 3: Fermentation
Yeast is added to the cooled wort to begin fermentation. During this process, yeast consumes sugars and produces alcohol and CO2. Temperature control is critical - ales ferment at 60-75°F while lagers require 45-55°F.
