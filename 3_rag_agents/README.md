# Evaluation and Benchmarking

1. Introduction to BrewOpsHub: The future of AI in the brewery

- The Winder Brewery gets lots of emails about orders, inventory, equipment, and customer inquiries.
- We like making and drinking beer more than answering emails.
- So we're building an AI system to handle all our email.

2. BrewOpsHub AutoRespond

- Initial results look interesting.
- We need to auto-respond to people.
- Need to automatically ignore people asking for Danish Pilsner (horrible!).

3. BrewOpsHub Customer Service

- People also asking other questions. "Where's my beer!"
- Current system isn't very "intelligent".

---

## Goal

- Incorporate information from other systems and sources
- Allow BrewOpsHub to access and use this information
- Make BrewOpsHub more "intelligent"

---

## What we need to know

- How do we ingest other sources of information?
- How can LLMs use this information?
- What do we mean by "intelligent"?
- How do we build more autonomous systems?
