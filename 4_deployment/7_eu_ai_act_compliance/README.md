## Understanding Your Obligations

**Minimal Risk (No obligations):**

- Internal tools, personal assistants
- General chatbots for entertainment
- Content generation for personal use

**Limited Risk (Transparency obligations):**

- Customer service chatbots
- Content generation for publication
- AI assistants that interact with people
- **Requirement**: Must inform users they're interacting with AI

**High Risk (Extensive obligations):**

- CV screening/recruitment tools
- Educational assessment/grading
- Medical diagnosis assistance
- Legal document analysis
- Credit scoring/financial decisions

**Prohibited (Cannot deploy):**

- Social scoring systems
- Emotion recognition in workplace/education (with exceptions)
- Subliminal manipulation systems

---

## Practical Compliance Implementation

**Compliance Checklist:**

1. Risk assessment: Determine your app's risk level
2. Transparency: Properly disclose AI use
3. Documentation: Track and store required information
4. Model Cards: Use provider documentation
5. Audit trails: Maintain logs for accountability
